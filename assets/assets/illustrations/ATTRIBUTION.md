# Illustration plates

Original period illustrations, placed at the matching scenes by `tool/build_illustrations.py`
(manifest: `tool/illustrations_manifest.json`). Each file was checked on Wikimedia Commons on Oct 12, 2026,
where it is marked **Public domain**. The artists died long ago (Hugh Thomson 1920, C. E. Brock 1938,
Theodor von Holst 1844) and the works were published 1831–1895, so they are public domain in the US
(published before 1931) and in life+70/life+100 countries. Images are faithful scans of 2D works.
