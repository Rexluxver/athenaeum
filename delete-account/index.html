Delete your account

Liber et Libri: Classics Reader (iOS, Android and web) · operated by Lux et Libri

You can delete your account and its data at any time. Deletion is permanent. We cannot restore an account afterwards.

Option 1: in the app (fastest)

  1. Open the app and sign in.
  2. Tap the account icon (top right of the bookcase) and choose Account.
  3. Scroll to Delete account and tap it.
  4. Read what will be deleted, then type DELETE (and your password, if you signed up with email) to confirm.

Teachers who own classes choose first whether to transfer them to a co-teacher, archive them, or delete them with all student work.

Option 2: request deletion by email

If you can't open the app or sign in, email us from the address on your account:

Email a deletion request

Write to privacy@luxetlibri.com with the subject “Delete my account”, and tell us your account email and how you sign in. We may ask you to reply from that address so we can confirm the account is yours. We complete requests within 30 days, usually much sooner, and confirm by email.

  • Class accounts (students): a parent, guardian, or the school can ask us, or ask the teacher. Include the class code and the student's first name. Students who can sign in can also delete the account themselves in the app.
  • Custom print orders: include the order email if it differs from your account email.

What is deleted

  • Your profile and sign-in (email address, Apple or Google link, display name).
  • Reading progress, bookmarks, notes, saved lines, streaks, ducats, quiz results and synced settings.
  • Class membership, writing and submissions (students), and your school or Google Classroom connection, which is also revoked at the provider.
  • Your subscription record in our systems and the matching customer record at RevenueCat.

What we keep, and for how long

  • Print-order records (book, edition, quantity, payment reference) for our business and tax records. Your name, email, shipping address and notes are removed once the order has shipped or been cancelled (within 30 days after), and immediately if it already has.
  • Anonymous server logs: Sentinel usage logs contain no question text and no identity after deletion, and are deleted after 30 days.
  • Backups roll off on a rolling schedule; deleted data is not restored into the live service.
  • Records held by your school (for example grades in its learning management system) follow the school's own policy.
  • Anything we must keep to comply with law or to resolve disputes, only for as long as required.

Subscriptions are not cancelled automatically

Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store or Google Play subscription. Cancel it yourself so you are not charged again:
  • Manage subscriptions on the App Store (iPhone/iPad: Settings › your name › Subscriptions)
  • Manage subscriptions on Google Play (Play Store › profile › Payments & subscriptions)
